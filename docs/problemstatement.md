# Problem Statement: Mutual Fund FAQ Assistant (Facts-Only Q&A)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Core Principle:** This assistant prioritizes accuracy over intelligence. It must ensure that users receive only verified, source-backed financial information, without any advisory bias, speculative content, or investment recommendations.

---

## 1. Overview

The objective of this project is to build a **facts-only FAQ assistant for HDFC Mutual Fund schemes**, using **Groww** as the reference product context and **HDFC Mutual Fund** as the selected AMC. 

The assistant is designed to answer objective, verifiable queries related to mutual funds by retrieving information exclusively from official public sources—such as Asset Management Company (**AMC**) websites, **AMFI** (Association of Mutual Funds in India), and **SEBI** (Securities and Exchange Board of India). 

The system must strictly avoid providing investment advice, opinions, or recommendations. Every response must include a single, clear source link and adhere to defined constraints around clarity, accuracy, and compliance.

---

## 2. Objective

Design and implement a lightweight **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**-based assistant that:
- Answers **factual queries** about mutual fund schemes.
- Uses a **curated corpus** of official public documents.
- Provides **concise, source-backed responses** without speculative or advisory content.

---

## 3. Target Users

- **Retail Investors:** Users comparing mutual fund schemes and seeking factual details (e.g., NAV, expense ratio, exit loads).
- **Customer Support & Content Teams:** Teams handling repetitive mutual fund queries who require quick, verified, and source-linked answers.

### 4.2. FAQ Assistant Requirements
The assistant must reliably answer objective, verifiable queries, such as:
- **Basic Scheme Details:** Expense ratio, exit load details, minimum SIP amount, ELSS lock-in period.
- **Risk & Classification:** Riskometer classification, benchmark index, risk profile (e.g., Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive).
- **Performance & Valuation:** Net Asset Value (NAV), last 3 years returns.
- **Portfolio & Management:** Scheme asset split (Equity vs. Debt vs. Hybrid), fund manager details, top holding details.
- **Tools & Processes:** SIP/Lumpsum Calculator functionality (strictly for *educational purposes only* for a given scheme), process to download account statements or capital gains reports.

#### Response Formatting Rules
Every successful response generated by the assistant **must** adhere to the following strict structure:
1. **Length Limitation:** Maximum of **3 sentences**.
2. **Citation:** Exactly **one citation link** pointing to the official source document.
3. **Mandatory Footer:** Must conclude with the following exact format:
   ```text
   Last updated from sources: <date>
   ```

### 4.3. Refusal Handling
The assistant must proactively recognize and **refuse non-factual, subjective, or advisory queries**. 

#### Examples of Queries to Refuse
- *"Should I invest in HDFC Nifty 50 Index Fund?"*
- *"Which fund is better — HDFC Nifty 50 or HDFC Nifty Next 50?"*
- *"Is this a good time to start an SIP in HDFC Banking & Financial Services Fund?"*

#### Refusal Response Guidelines
When refusing a query, the assistant must:
- Be polite, professional, and clearly worded.
- Reinforce its boundary as a **facts-only assistant**.
- Provide a relevant educational reference link (e.g., an official AMFI or SEBI investor education resource).

### 4.4. User Interface (Minimal)
The solution must provide a clean, minimal, and intuitive interface containing:
- **Welcome Message:** Explaining the assistant's purpose and capabilities.
- **Example Questions:** Three clickable or visible sample queries to guide the user.
- **Prominent Disclaimer:** A clearly visible disclaimer stating:
  > **"Facts-only. No investment advice."**

## 5. System Constraints & Compliance

| Category | Constraint Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Data & Sources** | • Use **only official public sources** (AMC websites, AMFI, SEBI).<br>• **Strictly prohibited:** Third-party blogs, financial news portals, or aggregator websites. |
| **Privacy & Security** | • **Zero data collection:** Do not collect, store, or process Personally Identifiable Information (PII).<br>• **Prohibited fields:** PAN numbers, Aadhaar numbers, bank account numbers, OTPs, email addresses, or phone numbers. |
| **Content Restrictions** | • **No advisory content:** Absolutely no investment advice, buy/sell/hold recommendations, or opinionated commentary.<br>• **No comparisons/calculations:** Do not perform custom fund comparisons or future return projections.<br>• **Performance queries:** For any performance-related queries, provide a direct link to the official scheme factsheet. |
| **Transparency** | • Responses must remain short, factual, and independently verifiable.<br>• Every answer must include its source link and timestamp. |

## 8. Summary
