About
HDFC Gold ETF Fund of Fund Direct Plan Growth
HDFC Gold ETF Fund of Fund Direct Plan Growth is a Commodities Mutual Fund Scheme launched by HDFC Mutual Fund. This scheme was made available to investors on 10 Dec 1999. Arun Agarwal is the Current Fund Manager of HDFC Gold ETF Fund of Fund Direct Plan Growth fund. The fund currently has an Asset Under Management(AUM) of ₹9,34,238 Cr and the Latest NAV as of 03 Jul 2026 is ₹45.37.
The HDFC Gold ETF Fund of Fund Direct Plan Growth is rated High risk. Minimum SIP Investment is set to ₹100. Minimum Lumpsum Investment is ₹100. Exit load of 1%, if redeemed within 15 days.
