About
HDFC Diversified Equity All Cap Active FoF Direct Growth
HDFC Diversified Equity All Cap Active FoF Direct Growth is a Equity Mutual Fund Scheme launched by HDFC Mutual Fund. This scheme was made available to investors on 10 Dec 1999. Dhruv Muchhal is the Current Fund Manager of HDFC Diversified Equity All Cap Active FoF Direct Growth fund. The fund currently has an Asset Under Management(AUM) of ₹9,34,238 Cr and the Latest NAV as of 03 Jul 2026 is ₹10.17.
The HDFC Diversified Equity All Cap Active FoF Direct Growth is rated Very High risk. Minimum SIP Investment is set to ₹100. Minimum Lumpsum Investment is ₹100. Exit load of 1%, if redeemed within 1 year.

The scheme seeks to generate long-term capital appreciation/income by investing in units of domestic equity-oriented schemes based on varied market caps.
