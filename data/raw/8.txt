About
HDFC Nifty500 Multicap 50:25:25 Index Fund Direct Growth
HDFC Nifty500 Multicap 50:25:25 Index Fund Direct Growth is a Equity Mutual Fund Scheme launched by HDFC Mutual Fund. This scheme was made available to investors on 10 Dec 1999. Arun Agarwal is the Current Fund Manager of HDFC Nifty500 Multicap 50:25:25 Index Fund Direct Growth fund. The fund currently has an Asset Under Management(AUM) of ₹9,34,238 Cr and the Latest NAV as of 03 Jul 2026 is ₹10.12.
The HDFC Nifty500 Multicap 50:25:25 Index Fund Direct Growth is rated Very High risk. Minimum SIP Investment is set to ₹100. Minimum Lumpsum Investment is ₹100.
;
Investment Objective
The scheme seeks to generate returns that are commensurate (before fees and expenses) with the performance of the Nifty500 Multicap 50:25:25 Index, subject to tracking error.
