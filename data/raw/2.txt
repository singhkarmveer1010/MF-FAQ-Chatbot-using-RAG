About
HDFC BSE Sensex Index Fund Direct Growth
HDFC BSE Sensex Index Fund Direct Growth is a Equity Mutual Fund Scheme launched by HDFC Mutual Fund. This scheme was made available to investors on 10 Dec 1999. Arun Agarwal is the Current Fund Manager of HDFC BSE Sensex Index Fund Direct Growth fund. The fund currently has an Asset Under Management(AUM) of ₹9,34,238 Cr and the Latest NAV as of 03 Jul 2026 is ₹742.91.
The HDFC BSE Sensex Index Fund Direct Growth is rated Very High risk. Minimum SIP Investment is set to ₹100. Minimum Lumpsum Investment is ₹100. Exit load of 0.25% if redeemed within 3 days
;
Investment Objective
The scheme aims to generate returns that are commensurate with the performance of S&P BSE Sensex, subject to tracking errors.
